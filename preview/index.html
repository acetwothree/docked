Meet Docked

Watch anything in a floating window while you doodle, take notes, or play. Docked reshapes itself around your video so nothing is ever hidden underneath it.

📺

Turn on Picture-in-Picture

1. iOS Settings ▸ General ▸ Picture in Picture → turn on “Start PiP Automatically”.
2. Play a video full-screen in YouTube, Netflix, Hulu, Safari…
3. Swipe up to the Home Screen — the video becomes a floating window.
⧉

Place it & go

Tap ⧉ in the bar any time to pick where your video sits, then drag the floating window into the TV frame. That's it.
