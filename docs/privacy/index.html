Docked — Privacy Policy

Last updated: 4 September 2026

Docked does not collect, store, transmit, or share any personal information. There are no accounts, no analytics, no advertising, and no third-party trackers of any kind.

Data stored on your device

Your notes, drawings, game progress, scores, chip balance, and app settings are saved only in local storage on your iPhone. They never leave your device and are never sent to us or anyone else. Deleting the app removes them.

Picture in Picture

Docked reshapes its own interface around iOS's floating video window. It does not access, record, or receive the contents of that video, or of any other app.

Photos

If you tap Save on a drawing, iOS asks for permission to add that one image to your photo library. Docked only writes the image you chose to save; it does not read, browse, or upload your photos.

Purchases

"Docked Plus" is an optional subscription processed entirely by Apple through the App Store. Docked never sees your payment details. Apple's handling of that transaction is covered by Apple's Privacy Policy.

Children

Docked collects no data from anyone, including children.

Changes

If this policy ever changes, the updated version will be posted at this same address with a new date.
