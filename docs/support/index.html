Docked — Support

iPhone · iOS 17 or later

Docked is a companion screen for iPhone Picture in Picture. When a video is floating over your Home Screen, Docked gives you a tidy little dashboard to doodle, take notes, and play quick games around it.

Getting started

  1. Play a video in Safari, YouTube, Apple TV, or any app that supports Picture in Picture.
  2. Tap the Picture in Picture button so the video becomes a small floating window.
  3. Swipe to the Home Screen and open Docked. The floating video sits on top; Docked's layout stays clear of it.
  4. Drag the bottom edge of the on‑screen "TV" to size the reserved area to match your video.

Doodle, Notes, and every activity also work with no video playing.

Docked Plus

Docked is free. "Docked Plus" is an optional auto‑renewing subscription ($2.99/month, prices may vary by region) that unlocks a few extra activities. Manage or cancel it any time in Settings › Apple Account › Subscriptions on your iPhone. To restore a purchase on a new device, open the paywall in Docked (gear knob › Docked Plus) and tap Restore.

Privacy

Docked has no accounts and collects no data. Everything stays on your device. Full details in the Privacy Policy.
